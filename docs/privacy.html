Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

English

Summary

StickyPresenter does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It is a fully on-device macOS app with no accounts, no analytics, and no network connections.

Data We Collect

None. The app does not gather personal information, usage analytics, identifiers, or telemetry of any kind.

Your Content

The notes, timers, and teleprompter text you create stay on your Mac and are handled only in memory or local app storage. They are never uploaded to us or any third party.

Clipboard

When you choose "New from Clipboard" or paste into the teleprompter, the app reads the clipboard solely to place that text into a note or script on your device. Clipboard contents are not stored, logged, or transmitted.

Network

The app makes no network requests and contains no advertising or third-party SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For any privacy questions, contact mizzking75@gmail.com.
